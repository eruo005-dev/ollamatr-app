# B6 — Founder Personal-Brand Operating System (OllamaTR)

**Goal:** 10,000 Türkçe-AI-curious followers in 12 months, with measurable conversion into OllamaTR users, contributors, and KOBİ pilots.

**Operating principle:** The operator is not "the Ollama Turkey guy." The operator is **"the person who is making Türkiye sovereign on local AI."** That framing survives a C&D, survives a competitor, and survives a platform pivot. Everything below ladders to that one sentence.

---

## 1. Platform Strategy & Prioritization

### Tier 1 — Primary (80% of effort)

**LinkedIn (TR)** — *the conversion engine*
- **Why primary:** TR developers, CTOs of KOBİs, and bürokrat-tech crowd live here. LinkedIn is the only TR platform where a 35-year-old Anadolu factory IT manager and a 25-year-old İstanbul ML engineer scroll the same feed. KOBİ buying decisions originate here. LinkedIn TR has ~14M users; engagement on Türkçe AI content is under-supplied vs. demand.
- **Conversion math:** LinkedIn → ollamatr.com → install. Highest-intent traffic of any channel.

**X / Twitter** — *the credibility engine*
- **Why primary:** Global AI Twitter is where Merve Noyan, Eren Gölge, Hugging Face team, and TR diaspora ML researchers live. A single quote-tweet from @mervenoyan is worth 6 months of LinkedIn grind for international credibility. Also where "Ollama Inc. cease & desist" rumors would first surface — must be present to defend.
- **Conversion math:** X → GitHub stars → developer adoption → contributor pipeline.

### Tier 2 — Secondary (15% of effort)

**YouTube (long-form, Türkçe)** — *the SEO + trust engine*
- **Why:** "ollama türkçe kurulum" YouTube search is empty. First mover wins evergreen TR-language SEO. 10-15min tutorials become the artifact every blog post, podcast, and conference talk links back to. One good video = 18 months of compounding views.
- **Cadence:** 1 video every 2 weeks. Quality over volume.

### Tier 3 — Experimental (5% of effort)

**Bluesky** — *early-mover hedge*
- **Why:** TR tech community is migrating slowly. Cost of presence is near-zero (cross-post from X). If Bluesky becomes the post-Musk default for TR researchers, B6 already has the handle and the follower base.
- **Cadence:** Mirror X posts. No original content.

### Tier 4 — Skipped (with justification)

**TikTok** — **skip in Y1.**
- Reasoning: TikTok TR audience skews 16-24 lifestyle/entertainment. KOBİ buyers and dev hires don't convert here. Short-form vertical video production cost (good lighting, on-camera presence, daily cadence to satisfy algorithm) is incompatible with also building product. Revisit Q4 if 10K LinkedIn goal hits early.

**Instagram Reels** — skip. Same reasoning as TikTok.

**Threads** — skip. No TR AI community has formed there. Bluesky is the better hedge.

**Defense of prioritization:** A solo founder cannot win 5 platforms. The pattern that works in TR tech: **win LinkedIn for revenue, win X for credibility, use YouTube as the asset library both link to.** Everything else is vanity.

## 6. Build-in-Public Journal

**Frequency:** 1 long-form post / week. Friday morning. ~1500-2500 kelime TR (primary), İngilizce kısa özet altta.

**Depth:** Specific numbers, named decisions, screenshots of code/dashboards, named mistakes. The unmistakable signal of build-in-public: when reader can't tell if you're allowed to share this.

### Share vs. Hold

**Share freely:**
- Revenue numbers (after first paying customer)
- User counts, install counts, churn
- Pricing experiments and outcomes
- Roadmap and what got cut
- Hiring decisions (when applicable)
- Burn rate and runway
- Open source contributor stats
- Conference / podcast results
- Mistakes — *especially* mistakes

**Hold (until safe to share):**
- Specific customer names without their written consent
- Active legal correspondence (e.g., a live Ollama Inc. C&D negotiation)
- Specific competitor diss by name (use "a competitor" — let readers guess)
- Personal financial position outside the company
- Anything that breaches KVKK on customer data
- Investor conversations until termsheet

**The 24-hour rule:** Anything emotionally charged sits 24 hours in drafts before publish.

### Platform Choice — Recommended: **self-hosted blog on `blog.ollamatr.com`** + cross-post to LinkedIn long-form + Substack mirror

**Comparison:**

| | Substack | Beehiiv | Self-hosted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup time | 1 hour | 1 hour | 1 day |
| Cost | Free → 10% rev share if paid | Free up to 2.5K subs | ~$5/mo + dev time |
| Email deliverability | Excellent | Excellent (better than Substack) | Hard — needs Mailgun/Resend |
| SEO control | Weak (Substack subdomain dilutes) | Weak | Full |
| Brand independence | Low (platform owns audience) | Medium | Full |
| Türkçe SEO ranking | Weak | Weak | Full (with proper hreflang) |
| Export/portability | OK (CSV email list) | OK | Total |
| Lock-in risk | High | Medium | None |
| Fits "egemen AI" narrative | **Contradicts it** | Contradicts it | **Reinforces it** |

**Verdict:** **Self-hosted blog on `blog.ollamatr.com`** is the only option consistent with the "sovereignty" brand. A founder who lectures KOBİs about owning their AI stack cannot host their newsletter on Substack with a straight face. Use **Beehiiv as the email-delivery layer** (cheap, deliverable, owns no content) until self-hosted email pipeline matures. Mirror to LinkedIn newsletter for distribution. Skip Substack entirely — it dilutes brand domain authority.

**Stack:** Astro or Next.js (MDX) → Cloudflare Pages → Beehiiv for email → cross-post to LinkedIn long-form → archive RSS to Bluesky.

## 9. Crisis Playbook

### Scenario A — Ollama Inc. C&D ("Trademark Infringement")

**Probability:** Medium-high within 18 months if OllamaTR grows.

**Pre-emption (do now):**
1. Domain `ollamatr.com` ve trademark araştırması yap. Türkiye'de TÜRKPATENT'te "OllamaTR" başvuru yap (~3-5K TL).
2. README'de açık disclaimer: *"OllamaTR, Ollama Inc. tarafından ne sponsorludur ne onaylanmıştır. Açık kaynak Ollama projesinin Türkçe yerelleştirme katmanıdır."*
3. Alternatif marka adı yedek tut: **"Yerel"** veya **"Egemen AI"** — domain ve handle'ları al, kullanma.
4. Ollama Inc.'a Y1 başında **proaktif friendly outreach** gönder: "We're building a TR localization layer, here's what we're doing, here's how we credit upstream, want to coordinate?" — bu yazılı kanıt sonrasında "kötü niyetli" iddiasını çürütür.

**If C&D arrives:**

1. **İlk 24 saat:** Avukat ara (TR + US licensing avukatı). Hiçbir şey yayınlama. Hiçbir DM cevaplama.
2. **48-72 saat:** Sessiz kal. Panik yayını = brand suicide.
3. **Yayın hazırlığı — pre-written template:**

> **Başlık (TR):** Ollama Inc.'tan dün aldığım mektup ve cevabım
>
> Dün Ollama Inc.'tan resmi bir mektup aldım. OllamaTR ismi nedeniyle trademark endişesini ileten bir mektup. Mektubu paylaşmıyorum (hukukçumun tavsiyesi) ama şu üç şeyi açıkça söylüyorum:
>
> 1. **OllamaTR'nin kuruluşundan beri pozisyonu nettir:** Ollama açık kaynak projesinin TR topluluğuna yönelik bir yerelleştirme ve KOBİ paketidir. Üst kaynak her zaman atıfla anılmıştır.
>
> 2. **Üst kaynak ekibine saygım tamdır.** Bir taraftan global açık kaynak projesini koruma sorumluluğu, diğer taraftan yerel topluluğun ihtiyacını görmezden gelmek istemeyişim. Bu dengeyi birlikte konuşacağız.
>
> 3. **Ürün durmuyor.** Eğer marka adı değişmesi gerekirse, [Y/N tarihinde] yeni isimle devam ederiz. Kullanıcıların hiçbir kaybı olmaz. Roadmap aynı. Söz veriyorum.
>
> Şeffaflık benim için savunulabilir bir konum. Bu yüzden olayın kendisini saklamak yerine açık konuşuyorum. Soruları yorum/DM'den cevaplayacağım.
>
> Bu hafta normal mesai. Salı yeni release var.

**Why this template works:** Sympathy without victim-playing. Respect to upstream (defuses "bad actor" framing). Continuity signal (defuses "this thing might die" customer panic). Names the worst case (rebrand) and minimizes it. Turkish stoic register, not American melodrama.

4. **Brand-name backup plan ready:** Within 30 days execute rename if necessary. "Yerel" or "EgemenAI" are pre-approved fallbacks. Domain redirects, GitHub repo rename, contributor migration — all scripted.

### Scenario B — Competitor Smear

**Likely vectors:** Anonymous TR tech Twitter account claims OllamaTR "steals from Ollama and dresses it up", or a competing TR AI tool's founder positions OllamaTR as "wrapper, not product."

**Response framework:**

1. **24-hour pause.** Never respond in anger.
2. **Diagnose:** Is the critique partially true? If yes, address the kernel of truth publicly. If no, ignore for 72 hours and let community defend.
3. **If response needed, use this template (X/LinkedIn):**

> X kişisinin OllamaTR hakkındaki [yorumunu / iddiasını] gördüm. Üç şey söyleyeyim:
>
> 1. **Doğru olan kısım:** [eğer bir doğruluk payı varsa, kabul et]. Bunu zaten roadmap'te [tarih] için planlıyorduk; şimdi öne çekiyoruz.
>
> 2. **Eksik olan kısım:** [veriyle düzelt — kullanıcı sayısı, contributor sayısı, KOBİ pilot sayısı, somut output].
>
> 3. **Önemli olan kısım:** Türk geliştirici ekosisteminin birbirine ihtiyacı var. X kişisinin ürününü saygıyla anıyorum [eğer rakipse, ismini ver, ürününü öv]. Pazar dar değil, geniş. Birbirimizi yıpratmamız hiçbirimize fayda getirmez.
>
> Devam, kod yazmaya geri dönüyorum.

**Why this works:** Owns flaws (kills credibility attack). Provides data (kills FUD). Generous to competitor (makes attacker look small). Closes with action signal (work, not drama).

### Scenario C — Customer KVKK Breach Accusation

**Pre-emption:**
- KVKK uyumluluk sayfası `ollamatr.com/kvkk` — hangi verinin nereye gittiği, OllamaTR'nin neden default offline çalıştığı.
- Hiçbir kullanıcı verisi telemetry'de toplama. Tamamen opt-in, ayrı sayfa.

**If accusation arrives:**
- Same playbook: 24-hour pause, gather facts, public response within 72 hours, name the specific KVKK article and OllamaTR's specific compliance.

### Scenario D — Personal Smear (LinkedIn or X)

Less likely than the above. Standard playbook: ignore for 48 hours, respond once with facts, never engage twice. Block, mute, move on.
